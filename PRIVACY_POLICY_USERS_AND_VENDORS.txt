Your Rights Your Choices

As a resident of California, you are granted specific rights under both federal and state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and its amendment, the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). These rights provide you with clear authority over your personal information, including the right to know what data is collected, request access, correct inaccuracies, and request deletion, subject to applicable limitations.

At ThePawffy, we are committed to honoring these rights and ensuring that you have transparent and accessible options to manage how your personal data is used, disclosed, and retained.

1. Right to Know
You have the right to obtain a report detailing the personal or business information collected, used, disclosed, or retained about you during the past 12 months.


2. Right to Access
You may access your account data, profile details, transaction history, or service listings associated with your profile at any time.


3. Right to Correct
You can request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete information related to your user or vendor account.


4. Right to Delete
You may request deletion of your data, subject to legal and operational retention requirements (e.g., fraud prevention, tax reporting).


5. Right to Limit the Use of Sensitive Information
You can request restriction on the use of sensitive data such as geolocation, identification documents when such data is not essential to delivering platform services.


6. Right to Opt Out of Data Disclosure 
You have the right to refuse the sale or sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted marketing.

We may allow third-party tools to collect limited data (such as, IP address, location, or device ID) using cookies. You can opt out via: Our Privacy Management Center(link).


7. Right to Appeal
In case your privacy request is denied, you have the right to appeal the decision. We will evaluate your appeal and provide a written explanation of our final determination.

8. Right to Non-Discrimination
You will not be denied access, penalized, or treated unfairly for exercising your privacy rights. Your experience on the platform will remain fully available regardless of your data preferences.
